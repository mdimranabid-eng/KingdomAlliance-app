## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive, institutional aesthetic built for high-stakes governance, global alliance administration, and philanthropic oversight. It pairs the architectural gravitas of deep majestic navy with the refined authority of subtle champagne gold accents.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Statuesque:** Grounded in timeless governance principles, communicating durability, transparency, and high fiduciary responsibility.
- **Executive Polish:** Uncluttered viewports, precise information density, purposeful data hierarchies, and zero gratuitous decoration.
- **Sovereign & Dignified:** Measured use of gold accents to communicate distinction and prestige without venturing into gaudy ornamentation.

### Target Audience
Global alliance directors, regional chapter overseers, enterprise partner managers, and operational directors commanding distributed global networks, mission initiatives, and complex cross-organizational datasets.

### Design Movement
**Corporate Modernism infused with Classical Sovereign Nuance:** Structural discipline driven by clean slate surfaces and high-contrast navy containers, elevated by hairline warm-neutral dividers, precise metric cards, and deliberate champagne gold focus anchors.

## Colors

The system employs an authoritative tri-tone structure supported by pristine cool neutrals and functional semantic indicators for critical governance data states.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Navy (`#0B192C`):** The commanding base for sidebars, persistent global navigation shells, high-contrast modal headers, and primary executive actions.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37` / Tint `#E5C07B`):** Reserved strictly for active indicators, tier distinctions, premium verification badges, primary focus rings, and milestone achievements. Never used for large body surfaces.
- **Tertiary Maritime Blue (`#1E3E62`):** Used for interactive hovering states, secondary data groupings, and table header accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A`):** Ultra-high contrast text typography ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against slate backdrops.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) creates a luminous, soft plane that eliminates eye strain in prolonged operations.
- **Elevated Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) houses discrete modular widgets, metric counters, and data tables.
- **Structural Lines:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) defines clean borders and tabular dividers without visual weight.

### Data Visualization & Status Anchors
- **Success / Healthy:** `#059669` (Emerald 600) — Member compliance, thriving initiative metrics.
- **Cautionary / Pending:** `#D97706` (Amber 600) — Alliance charter updates awaiting ratification.
- **Urgent / Inactive:** `#E11D48` (Rose 600) — Escalated issues, stalled sponsorships.
- **Strategic / Special Initiatives:** `#7C3AED` (Violet 600) — Cross-continental summit data, executive summits.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and high-level structural titles with **Inter** for high-density administrative tabular data, complex form controls, and multi-tier lists.

### Typography Guidelines
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its balanced geometric structure, subtle curvature, and authoritative poise. Keep letter tracking tighter on larger weights to evoke architectural solidity.
- **Body & Tabular (`Inter`):** Deployed for high scanning speed and legibility at scale. Form inputs, data tables, analytical readouts, and chapter rosters utilize `Inter` with native tabular lining figures enabled (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'cv05' on`).
- **Overlines & Section Labels (`label-sm`):** Render in full uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.06em`) to frame table panels and metric clusters with executive rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an authoritative **12-column fluid grid system** anchored within a maximum bounded width of `1600px` for optimal executive visual field control.

### Spacing Scale
Built strictly on a foundational 4px base (`0.25rem`), advancing to 8px intervals for layout increments:
- Internal component spacing leverages `gutter-sm` (8px) and `gutter-md` (16px).
- Widget gap separation and dashboard card grids consistently employ `gutter-lg` (24px).
- Structural dashboard canvas sections split along `gutter-xl` (32px) margins.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (`>= 1280px`):** Full 12-column layout with fixed persistent 280px left navigation rail. High-density grids display up to 4 metric cards per row (`col-span-3`).
- **Tablet (`768px - 1279px`):** Grid condenses to 8 dynamic columns with the navigation rail collapsing into an icon-only 80px bar. Analytics dashboards fold cards into 2 per row (`col-span-4`).
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Single-column stack with 16px screen margins (`margin-mobile`). The navigation converts to an off-canvas drawer accessed via an executive header app bar.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cloud shadows, selecting instead **crisp card elevation** driven by delicate hairline borders and micro-calibrated ambient shadows tinted slightly with deep navy pigment.

### Hierarchy Tiers
- **Flat Surface (Level 0):** Used for base dashboard canvas background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Card Surface (Level 1):** Main data containers, KPI metric blocks, and tables. Defined by a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) reinforced with a micro-drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Interactive Card (Level 2):** Applied when hovering over actionable alliance member profiles or interactive analytical slices: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`, accompanied by a subtle transition of the border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Flyout & Contextual Dropdowns (Level 3):** Global search results, executive filter panels, and date range pickers: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **System Modals & Critical Dialogs (Level 4):** Center-staged oversight modals flanked by a 40% opacity Navy backdrop backdrop blur (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.40)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`): `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry. Clean lines with restrained edge treatments convey institutional stability, structural discipline, and architectural authority.

### Radii Application
- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px):** Form text inputs, select elements, small tag badges, pagination controls, and tooltips.
- **Card & Panel Elements (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Metric dashboards, summary containers, interactive charts, and standard dialog windows.
- **Major Overlay Sheets (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem / 12px):** Governance charter review modals and primary enterprise slide-over trays.
- **Exception (Full Pill):** Status indicators, chapter health chips, and member tier tags use `rounded-full` to stand out against structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** High-density Navy background (`#0B192C`) with pure white text, hairline border (`#1E3E62`), and 0.25rem radius. Hover state shifts to Maritime Navy (`#1E3E62`). Active focus produces a 2px offset ring in Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Secondary / Subtle:** White background with 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Executive Accent (Gold Highlight):** Used exclusively for high-tier initiatives, endorsements, and charter ratification: Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) background, dark navy typography (`#0B192C`), semi-bold.

### Input Fields
- Built on a 40px standard administrative height with an interior padding of 12px horizontally.
- Slate border (`#CBD5E1`) over `#FFFFFF` canvas. 
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0B192C` with a 2px outer aura in tinted Champagne Gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`). Never display raw browser outlines.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Compact visual chips using `label-sm` font attributes with `padding: 2px 8px`.
- High-status alliances use an outline treatment: transparent background with 1px border in the semantic tone (e.g., Emerald `#059669` for active global standing, Amber `#D97706` for under review).
- Global Tier Badges: Navy fill with gold foil lettering (`#E5C07B`) for founding member designations.

### Data Tables & Rosters
- Striping is omitted; clean visual pacing is created through 1px horizontal dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- Header rows utilize an off-slate ground (`#F8FAFC`) with uppercase tracking labels (`label-sm`).
- Hover row highlights employ `#F8FAFC` to emphasize cursor tracking through high-density international partner lists.

### Executive KPI Metric Cards
- Grounded on `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 elevation and an 8px radius.
- Structural anatomy: Subdued overline category label, followed by a primary numeric metric in `headline-lg` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), anchored at the base with an inline trend vector (percentage shift colored via semantic Emerald or Rose) and comparative timeline contextualizer.
